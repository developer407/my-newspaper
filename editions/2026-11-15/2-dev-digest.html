Spring's new 'Patch Thursday': one-day release train, 160+ CVEs fixed since March

Spring Blog · Sep 21, 2026 (catch-up; sets the calendar for the coming weeks)

The Spring release train now lands in a single day — the Thursday after the third Monday of each month — instead of a two-week rollout. The team says it averages nearly 80 community security reports a month since March, has fixed over 160 new CVEs and published 91 advisories in one month; spring.io/security was redesigned to filter by CVE, severity and project.

💡 Why it matters to you: Plan monthly dependency bumps around one date: by the stated rule (third Monday = Nov 16) the next train is Thursday Nov 19 — my inference from the rule, not an announced date.
Read deeper

The first train in the new format was Oct 22, 2026 (Spring Boot 4.1.2/4.0.9, Spring Security 7.1.2/7.0.8, Spring Framework 7.0.10, Spring AI 2.0.2 and others all shipped that day). Sept 24 was a milestone-only train. The post stresses that CVEs are now expected to be exploited within hours, so slow patching is riskier than it used to be.

A sensible pipeline: let Dependabot/Renovate open PRs on Thursday, run your Testcontainers suite, and merge by the following week. Use the new security site to filter advisories for the projects you actually depend on.
