Java and Spring

Java4Shell (CVE-2026-114938): a pre-auth RCE in the OpenJDK Base64 decoder, patch your JVMs

Sysdig, BleepingComputer, Semgrep, Spring blog · disclosed Nov 17, 2026

Researchers at Calif disclosed a memory-corruption flaw in the Base64 decoder of OpenJDK's HotSpot JVM, tracked as CVE-2026-114938 and nicknamed Java4Shell, with a reported CVSS 4.0 score of 9.2. They reported unauthenticated code execution on Jenkins, Keycloak, JFrog Artifactory, PingFederate, Confluence and Jira. Oracle and OpenJDK shipped fixes on Nov 17, and Josh Long told Spring developers to update their JVMs.

💡 Why it matters to you: Your Spring Boot services run on this JVM, so the fix is a runtime upgrade, not a library bump: rebuild your Docker or Jib images on a patched JDK.
Read deeper

One source lists fixed builds as 17.0.21.1, 21.0.13.1, 25.0.5.1 and 27.0.1.1, and says Java 8 and 11 are reportedly not on the vulnerable path. Sources differ on the exact affected ranges and vendor build numbers, so check Oracle, your distribution (Red Hat lists RHSA-2026:100567) or your base image notes. One source found no confirmed active exploitation when it checked.

If you cannot update right away, the reported temporary mitigation is to restart the JVM with the Base64 intrinsic disabled. In containers, set it through JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS or your Jib jvmFlags, and confirm the running version with java -version inside the image.

# temporary mitigation until you can patch (reported by Dynatrace and others)
JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS=-XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics
# or
java -XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics -jar app.jar

OpenID4VP accepts a forged certificate chain: a lesson in X.509 trust-anchor validation

GitHub Advisory Database · Nov 19, 2026

@openeudi/openid4vp (CVE-2026-75522, high) accepted an X.509 chain that used a forged certificate carrying a trusted anchor's subject name. A forged cert could pass without being signed by the real trust anchor.

💡 Why it matters to you: If you verify JWTs or certificates in Spring Security or jjwt, validate signatures against the anchor's key, never just match names.
Read deeper

The advisory is about verifiable-credential presentation, not JWT, but the rule carries over to any OAuth2 or JWT stack: trust is established by cryptographic verification, not by an issuer or subject string. See last edition's pac4j-jwt lessons for the same theme.

PostgreSQL 19 is out: REPACK CONCURRENTLY, parallel autovacuum and faster foreign key checks

PostgreSQL Global Development Group and Phoronix · Nov 19, 2026

PostgreSQL 19 is generally available. It adds REPACK with a nonblocking CONCURRENTLY option, parallel index vacuuming through autovacuum_max_parallel_workers, faster foreign key checks (bulk inserts nearly 2x faster in testing) and removes RADIUS authentication. SQL/PGQ property graph support was reverted before release.

💡 Why it matters to you: Your pgvector and JPA workloads get lower maintenance pain: you can rebuild bloated tables without long locks, and replicas get read-your-writes through WAIT FOR LSN.
Read deeper

Other items from the beta notes: the pg_plan_advice extension for plan stability, logical replication of sequences (helpful for online upgrades) and LZ4 as the default TOAST compression when built with LZ4. Test your pgvector build against 19 before upgrading, since I found no pgvector compatibility notice.

Pre-release coverage also said some headline features were pulled during beta and the release is smaller than planned.
